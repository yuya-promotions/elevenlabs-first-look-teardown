# ElevenLabs: a Growth PM's first-week teardown

I tried ElevenLabs on the free plan (10,000 credits) as a new user and wrote down
what helped me, what stopped me, and what I would test first.

## What I built
- Automatic Dubbing: I dubbed a Japanese creator’s short video about a camera lens into English as a private test (not published), to see how ElevenLabs could help localize creator content for overseas markets.

## What worked
- The dubbed English voice kept the speaker's tone remarkably well.
- Examples show the prompt behind each output. Seeing how, not just the result,
  made me think "I can do this too."

## Where I got stuck
| Where | What happened | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | With 10K credits and many products, I couldn't tell what to try first, or which features the free plan blocks or that 10K credits can't cover. | New users who don't find a first win in session one rarely come back. |
| Product Showcase Video | All 6 Product Showcase Video examples stuttered around 0:03 and 0:06. | Same symptom across all 6 points to one shared cause, and examples are where users decide to try. |
| Export | The dubbed video had a watermark I only noticed after export, and it looked clearly lower-resolution than the source. Only later, from the pricing page and docs, did I learn that free dubs are watermarked and that the video is a preview meant for swapping the dubbed audio into the original. Nothing at export said so, and I couldn't tell whether paying would give me a video at the original resolution that I could use as-is. | When users can't see what paying gets them, they hesitate to upgrade, or upgrade with the wrong expectation and churn. |

## What I would test first
1. **"Your first 10K credits" starter path** — 3 to 5 recommended first projects, each with its credit cost, plan requirement, and free-plan limits (such as the dubbing watermark) shown up front.
   Metric: share of new sign-ups who complete a first output in week 1.
2. **Show free-plan limits before the dub, not after** — e.g. "Free: watermark included" next to the dub button, against a control with no notice.
   Metric: free-to-paid conversion and 60-day paid retention, since hiding limits may lift upgrades but cost trust.
3. **Explain the output and the paid value at export** — say the video is a preview, offer "download the dubbed audio" as the next step, and list what upgrading adds (no watermark, commercial use) and what stays the same.
   Metric: free-to-paid conversion among users who export a dub, and churn in the first paid month.
4. **Fix the showcase playback** — check the shared cause (encoding, preload, or player).
   Metric: example play-through rate and "try this" clicks.

## About me
Growth and lifecycle marketing leader at Sony (Japan, China, India). I once lifted
online class video completion 3x by changing camera tutorials from showing sample
photos to showing the exact menu settings behind them. That is why ElevenLabs
showing the prompt behind each example stood out to me.
